Workflow contract

01

Load

Bring the source signal into a predictable structure.

02

Normalise + match

Standardise fields and preserve unmatched issues rather than hiding them.

03

Classify + gate

Separate routine outcomes from critical or uncertain cases that require human review.

04

Generate

Produce a reviewable summary and draft output, not an unverified final decision.
