CASE STUDY / 03GOVERNANCEEXPLORING

Make the mapping reusable.

A concept for turning data dictionary and source-to-target mapping work into durable documentation instead of one-off explanations.

The opportunity

Mapping decisions are often repeated across tickets, handovers and validation sessions. A reusable mapping surface could make assumptions, transformations and ownership easier to find.

Design boundary

  • Suggest structure, never invent business meaning
  • Keep source evidence beside each mapping
  • Leave uncertain mappings visible for human review
