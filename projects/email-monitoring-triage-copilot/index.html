Turn monitoring noise into reviewable action.

Email Monitoring Triage Copilot explores how recurring monitoring messages can become a deterministic, structured workflow without hiding uncertainty or critical exceptions.

What it demonstrates

  • Normalisation of inconsistent monitoring inputs
  • Rule-based matching and classification
  • Explicit review-needed outputs
  • Response drafting with human control

Why it matters

The value is not simply generating an email faster. It is making the path from signal to judgement visible and repeatable.
